I'm Gabe Yocum, an applied and computational mathematics student at BYU.

pipelines and agents that take tedious work off people's plates.

On the side I ship a steady stream of projects, like a music review network
and an iOS app making its way to the web. They run on a single free ARM
server, which I [wrote about](/posts/hello-world/).

I like problems where the math and the engineering meet. If you have one of
those, or just want to talk shop, my inbox is open.
