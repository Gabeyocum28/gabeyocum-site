Office hours with a 3D avatar that has read the syllabus. Upload the course
materials, then talk to it out loud over a real call interface.

The avatar is rendered in Three.js and lip-syncs to speech generated from the
model's replies. Course material is chunked and embedded so answers cite the
actual lecture notes instead of guessing.
